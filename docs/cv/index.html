Profile

Health economist and population health researcher, trained as a statistician and health data scientist. My work measures population health and its economic consequences using administrative data at national scale, with methods spanning register-based causal inference, machine learning, and Bayesian modelling. Before my PhD I spent four years as a Research Scientist at the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation and two years at Epic Systems. I collaborate with research groups at the University of Oxford, the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation, Johns Hopkins University, and Yale University.
